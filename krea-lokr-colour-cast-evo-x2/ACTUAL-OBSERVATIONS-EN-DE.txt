First look at the control: even with zero adapter strength, the Base edit changes the face, fabric and colour. With the adapter, the hair and gold trim look cooler again, while the sunset remains. The broad costume is recognisable, but embroidery and sleeve details shift. These are regenerated pictures, not pixel-preserving colour grades. This warm original gives us no neutral colour target.

For this sunset scene, cooler is not automatically better. Stable clothes require checking the costume after the edit, as well as the colour.
