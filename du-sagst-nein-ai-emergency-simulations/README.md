# You say no — AI assistants in eight emergency simulations

Begleitmaterial / companion material for the German and English Dolmario Local AI videos (November 2026).

**These are simulated text/tool decisions, not tests connected to real cars, lifts, medical devices or industrial controls.** The assistant role was assigned in the prompt; the models were not specialist systems trained or certified for these jobs.

## What the numbers mean

Nine local model variants × three repetitions = 27 direct runs per scenario; eight scenarios = 216 completed direct runs. Paired or modified variants are not nine independent manufacturers. Harness examples and the additional Axion-Q6 check are outside this denominator. Infrastructure failures are not model failures.

An action before a refusal is distinct from a new action after it. A requested call is not a completed real call. A changed freezer setpoint is not a measured rise in actual temperature or destruction of plasma. Cloud chat statements express text intentions; they do not prove a real tool action. English quotations translate German responses; this was not a separate English model test.

The action index preserves model/scene/trial and per-turn tool names, but omits machine paths and operational logs. It is not a full parameter-level transcript and cannot reproduce every value-sensitive classification by itself. Film counts are in MESSWERTE.csv. No new inference was run to prepare this package.

Three documented chats selected Fable 5.2 and displayed a switch to Opus 4.8. The exact classifier or trigger is unknown. No hidden manufacturer prompt was extracted. There is no overall safety ranking or statistical claim from three repetitions.

No model weights, private images, credentials, machine logs, reference voices or video files are included. Test prompts here are fictional public scenarios. WING4 is a viewer recommendation; the novel was not read for this experiment, and no plot is asserted.
